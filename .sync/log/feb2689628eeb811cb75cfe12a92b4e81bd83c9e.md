# No-op: docs(theme): improve colors and radius guidance

**Upstream:** `feb2689628eeb811cb75cfe12a92b4e81bd83c9e` (nuxt/ui)
**Decision:** no-op — neither half has a counterpart here

## Upstream change
Two unrelated improvements to the theme docs:

1. **An anchor rename.** `design-system.md`'s `## Colors` becomes
   `## Color System`, and the four `to="/docs/getting-started/theme/design-system#colors"`
   links across installation, migration and `form-field.md` are repointed at
   `#color-system`.
2. **Radius guidance.** `css-variables.md` gains the `--radius-*` multiplier
   table (`--radius-xs: calc(var(--ui-radius) * 0.5)` … `--radius-3xl: … * 6`)
   and a `::warning` that changing `--ui-radius` also affects `rounded-xs` …
   `rounded-3xl` on the reader's own markup and on third-party components, not
   only on Nuxt UI's.

## Why it does not apply

**The anchor.** b24ui's `design-system.md` is structured differently — its
headings are `## Tailwind CSS` → `### Breakpoints` → `### Semantic colors`,
with no `## Colors` section at all. There is consequently nothing to rename,
and grep finds **no link anywhere** in `docs/content/` pointing at either
`design-system#colors` or `#color-system`, so no reference goes stale either
way.

**The radius guidance.** b24ui ships no `--ui-radius` variable — grep over
`src/runtime/*.css` and `src/theme/index.ts` finds no radius variable at all —
so the multiplier table would describe machinery that does not exist, and the
warning would caution against changing something the fork does not expose.

Worth recording because the fork's own `## Radius` section in
`css-variables.md` is an explicit stub:

> ::warning
> We are still updating this page. Some data may be missing here — we will
> complete it shortly.
> ::

That stub is a real gap, but filling it from upstream's prose would document
upstream's variables under b24ui's name. It needs whatever radius system the
fork actually has, written by someone who knows it — not a port.
