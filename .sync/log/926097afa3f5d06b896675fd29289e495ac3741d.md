# n/a — nuxt/ui@926097afa3f5d06b896675fd29289e495ac3741d

**Upstream:** fix(CheckboxGroup/RadioGroup): lift a hovered table item above its neighbors (#7073)

**Decision:** n/a.

In upstream's `table` variant a hovered item switches its border to
`border-accented` (the `highlight: false` compound variant), and since the items
overlap by one pixel (`-space-y-px`) the neighbour painted on top hid half of
that border. The fix adds `hover:z-[1]` to the hovered item and bumps the
checked item from `z-[1]` to `z-[2]`, so a checked item still wins over a
hovered neighbour.

The fork's `checkbox-group.ts` and `radio-group.ts` themes are their own
(Bitrix24 `--ui-color-design-outline-na-*` tokens): the `table` item carries no
hover border change at all, and there is no `highlight` compound variant on the
item. A hovered item looks the same as an idle one, so there is no border for a
neighbour to hide and nothing to lift. The checked item keeps its `z-[1]`, which
already sits above idle and hovered neighbours alike.

Whoever adds a hover border to these themes later should take both halves of
this commit together (`hover:z-[1]` plus checked `z-[2]`), or the checked item
will tie with a hovered neighbour.
