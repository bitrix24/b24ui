## Why this is not a copy

The **shape** transfers, the **values** cannot: a badge here has to describe
b24ui's release train, not Nuxt UI's. Each was checked against this repo's tags
(`git tag --contains <sha>`) rather than assumed to match upstream's timing.

| upstream | here | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Soon` → `4.11+` on `componentDetection` | `Soon` → **`2.12+`** | `530b9616` (#396) is in no tag before `v2.12.0` |
| drop `New` from `input-rating.md` | dropped | `d08b5830` (#283) shipped in `v2.10.0` — two releases back, so the badge was stale here too |
| `Soon` → `New` on `splitter.md` | **added** `navigation.badge: New` | `62a2acc8` (#441) first appears in `v2.12.0`; our page never had a badge to change |
| `Soon` → `New` on `progress-group.md` | **added** `navigation.badge: New` | same, `14ac2438` (#443) |
| remove the `::note` "Soon" badges | nothing to remove | the notes were ported in `07f3fe8d` **without** the badge, precisely because this commit deletes it two commits later |

Upstream also normalises `navigation:\n  badge: Soon` to the flat
`navigation.badge: New` on `progress-group.md`; ours is written flat from the
start, matching `input-rating.md`, which was the only page in this fork using the
key.

Not touched: `calendar.md`, `drawer.md`, `modal.md`, `slideover.md` and the two
`With external scroll element` badges. They are this fork's own "Soon"/"New"
markers on sections upstream's commit does not mention, and their timing is a
separate question from this port.
