## Upstream change
One sentence in `docs/content/docs/2.components/tabs.md`:

```diff
-Set the `content` prop to `false` to turn the Tabs into a toggle-only control without displaying any content. Defaults to `true`.
+Set the `content` prop to `false` to render the triggers without any panels. Defaults to `true`.
```

The old wording described the *use case* ("toggle-only control") rather than
the effect, and "without displaying any content" is ambiguous about whether the
panels are hidden or never rendered.

## b24ui port
b24ui's page carried the old sentence byte-for-byte, and the prop means the
same thing here — `Tabs.vue` declares it as *"The content of the tabs, can be
disabled to prevent rendering the content"*, `@defaultValue true`, so the
panels genuinely are not rendered rather than hidden. Applied verbatim.

Note the fork also has an unrelated per-item `content?: string` prop on
`TabsItem`; the sentence is about the root-level boolean, and the reworded
version is no more ambiguous between the two than the original was.
