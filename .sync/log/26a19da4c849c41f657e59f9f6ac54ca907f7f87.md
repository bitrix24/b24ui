## Why it does not apply

`docs/content/showcase.yml` exists here, and that is the whole reason this needs
a sentence rather than a shrug: the *file* is a counterpart, the *content* is not.
Upstream's `items:` is a directory of third-party sites built with their library.
Ours holds a single placeholder — `SomeCase`, pointing at
`https://www.bitrix24.com/tools/crm/`.

Fontnode is built with nuxt/ui, not with `@bitrix24/b24ui`. Adding it here would
be a false claim about this library's users, which is the opposite of what a
showcase is for. There is no b24ui equivalent to add in its place, and inventing
one is not a port.
