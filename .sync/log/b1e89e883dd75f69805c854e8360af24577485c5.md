## Upstream change
- **Catalog:** `@nuxt/kit`, `@nuxt/schema` and `nuxt` go from `^4.5.2` to `^4.6.0`. The `@nuxt/kit` override moves with them.
- **`minimumReleaseAgeExclude`:** seven entries for the nuxt 4.6.0 family and `@nuxt/cli` 4.0.0.
- **Lockfile:** regenerated.
- **`useFetch` / `useLazyFetch`:** `params:` → `query:` in 13 docs examples and 3 Nuxt playground pages.
- **`docs/app/middleware/releases.ts`:** narrows `to.params.tag` with `'tag' in to.params`. Typed route params are a per-route union under 4.6.
- **`docs/modules/component-example.ts`:** `buildStart` tells the client build apart by `this.environment.name`. Under the Vite Environment API, `build.ssr` is set for both builds.
- **`docs/nuxt.config.ts`:** `future.compatibilityVersion: 5`, plus `experimental.viteEnvironmentApi: false` until nuxt-content/nuxt-component-meta#128 ships.
- **`playgrounds/nuxt/nuxt.config.ts`:** `future.compatibilityVersion: 5`.

- **Other overrides, checked against `npm view nuxt@4.6.0` / `@nuxt/vite-builder@4.6.0` / `@nuxt/nitro-server@4.6.0`:**
  - `vite: ^8.3.2` and `rolldown: ~1.2.11` are unchanged. vite-builder 4.6.0 asks for `vite ^8.3.2`, which is what the override already says.

  - `nitropack: ^2.13.0` and `unplugin: ^3.3.0` are unchanged as specifiers. The lockfile stayed on nitropack 2.13.3 and unplugin 3.3.0, below nitro-server 4.6.0's `^2.13.4` and nuxt 4.6.0's `^3.4.0`. `pnpm update -r nitropack unplugin --lockfile-only` moved them to 2.13.4 and 3.4.0, upstream's resolutions.
  - **`unimport: 6.0.2` conflicts with nuxt 4.6 and is left in place.** nuxt 4.6.0 declares `unimport ^7.0.2` (4.5.2 declared `^6.4.0`, so the pin was already below range). The override forces it onto 6.0.2. Upstream has no such override and resolves both 6.4.0 and 7.0.2. Every name nuxt 4.6 imports from `unimport` exists in 6.0.2: `createUnimport`, `defineUnimportPreset`, `scanDirExports`, `toExports`, `toTypeDeclarationFile`, `toTypeReExports`. The full gate below passes with the pin. Whether to move it to 7.x or drop it is a maintainer decision and is not part of this port.
