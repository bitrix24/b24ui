# Port: chore(deps): update all non-major dependencies

**Batched with** `dd4bc8e8` (#6876) into one PR; contiguous, §6 4b.

## Upstream change

Renovate's non-major sweep across five manifests. A `chore(deps)` port bumps
only the packages where this fork already declares upstream's *old* version in
the *same* section — that is the rule that keeps a deliberate hold from being
dragged along, and it was applied by comparing upstream's manifests at this
commit's parent (`aa5f4af0`, our cursor) against ours, per package per section.

## What was ported

Where ours == upstream's pre-image, bumped to upstream's new pin:

- **root `package.json`** — `@tanstack/vue-virtual` 3.13.35→36, `@unhead/vue`
  3.3.2→3.4.0, `magic-string` 1.2.0→2, `vue-component-type-helpers` 3.3.10→11,
  and dev: `ai` 7.0.66→77, `eslint` 10.8.1→10.9.0, `happy-dom` 20.11.2→6,
  `vitest` 4.1.10→11, `vue-tsc` 3.3.10→11.
- **`docs/package.json`** — `@ai-sdk/vue` 4.0.66→77, `ai` 7.0.66→77, `joi`
  18.2.3→5, `vue-component-meta` 3.3.10→11.
- **`playgrounds/nuxt`** — `@ai-sdk/vue`, `ai`, `vue-tsc`.
- **`playgrounds/vue` / `playgrounds/repl`** — `vite` 8.2.1→2, `vue-tsc`.

## What was NOT ported

- **`nuxt-schema-org`** — upstream 6.2.9→6.3.0; we hold `^6.2.1`, the recorded
  `dep-parity` exception. Any bump drags `nuxt-site-config` to a version calling
  `@nuxt/kit` functions no published 4.x exports; it breaks `dev:prepare`, not
  install. Left at 6.2.1.
- **We-lack-it**: `@ai-sdk/anthropic`, `@ai-sdk/gateway`, `@iconify-json/lucide`,
  `@iconify-json/vscode-icons`, `@takumi-rs/core` — not declared in this fork's
  manifests (the AI-provider set here is deliberately narrower, and icons come
  from `@bitrix24/b24icons-vue`, not iconify). Nothing to bump.
