## Upstream change
Removes Tailwind classes that do not exist or that conflict with another class on the same slot. This came out of the new `better-tailwindcss` lint (nuxt/ui@1c25c62, the next commit):
- `editor.ts`: `[&_pre_code]:font-inherit` is not a Tailwind utility.
- `page-columns.ts`: `column-1` is a typo for `columns-1`.
- `prose/img.ts`: `will-change-opacity` is not a Tailwind utility.
- `separator.ts`: `align-center` is not a Tailwind utility.
- `stepper.ts`: `font-medium` conflicts with `font-semibold` on `trigger`.

Snapshots for AuthForm, Editor, PageColumns and Separator are updated.
