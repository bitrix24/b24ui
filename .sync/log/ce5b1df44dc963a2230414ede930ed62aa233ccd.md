# Port: fix(theme): respect reduced motion on remaining movement transitions

**Upstream:** `ce5b1df4` (nuxt/ui) — full SHA in the ledger
**Decision:** port — intent, with a wider sweep than upstream's

## Upstream change
`motion-reduce:transition-none` appended to every movement transition across 13
theme files: the rotating chevrons in `accordion`, `chat-reasoning`,
`chat-tool`, `navigation-menu`, `content-toc`, `content-navigation`,
`prose/code-tree`, `prose/collapsible` and `tree`; the hover-scale transforms in
`blog-post`, `changelog-version` and `user`; and the
`transition-[color,translate]` on `content-surround`'s leading icon.

It is the sibling of `735b264`, which this fork already ported: that one
neutralised **animations** under `prefers-reduced-motion`, this one neutralises
**transitions**. The distinction matters here, because b24ui implemented
`735b264` as a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block in
`keyframes.css` — and redefining keyframes does nothing for a
`transition-transform`. Chevrons kept rotating.

## b24ui port
Five of the 13 files do not exist here (`blog-post`, `changelog-version`,
`content/content-navigation`, `prose/code-tree`, `tree`). The remaining eight
carried the defect and are fixed.

**The sweep is wider than upstream's, on purpose.** The commit's title says
*remaining* — it completes a pass over upstream's own themes. Reproducing only
the eight shared slots would leave the fork's own components still moving under
reduced motion, which is the accessibility gap the commit exists to close.
Guarded here beyond upstream's list:

- `sidebar.ts` — `gap` and `container` slide the sidebar open
  (`transition-[width]`, `transition-[inset-inline-start,…]`);
- `tabs.ts` — the `indicator` slides between triggers
  (`transition-[translate,width]`);
- `switch.ts` — the thumb travels (`transition-transform`);
- `prose/code-group.ts` — same slide as the tabs indicator;
- `navigation-menu.ts` — the viewport resizes
  (`transition-[width,height,left,right]`).

**Deliberately left alone**, because they are not movement — upstream guards
movement and leaves colour transitions running, and so does this:

- `file-upload.ts:23` and `switch.ts:21` — `transition-[background]`;
- `switch.ts:43` — `transition-[color,opacity]`.

**Already guarded, differently:** `prose/collapsible.ts` writes
`motion-safe:group-data-[state=open]:rotate-180`, so under reduced motion the
rotation class is never applied and the transition has nothing to animate.
Adding `motion-reduce:transition-none` there would be redundant and would churn
a snapshot for no behavioural change, so it stays as it is. Worth knowing the
fork has both spellings.
