## The defect

`filterGroups` scores every item in a group, keeps structural items (labels,
separators) at score `-1`, and then sorted the whole group by score:

```ts
result.sort((a, b) => a.score - b.score)
```

A `-1` sorts first, so every label and separator was pulled to the top of its
group, and matches were free to cross them. In a `CommandPalette`, `InputMenu`
or `SelectMenu` group with sub-headings, typing reordered the headings away from
the items they head.

## The fix

Sort each run of matches between structural items on its own, and leave the
structural items where they were.

## Test-first

Upstream's `test/composables/useFilter.spec.ts` is new in this commit, and this
fork had no spec for `useFilter` at all. It was added **before** the source
change and run against the fork's code:

| case | before the fix |
| --- | --- |
| keeps labels and separators where they are while sorting the matches | **red** |
| does not let a match move across a label | **red** |

So the defect is real here, and the two green cases describe behaviour this
fork already had.
