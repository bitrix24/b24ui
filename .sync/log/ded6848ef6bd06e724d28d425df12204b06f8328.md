**Upstream:** fix(SelectMenu): keep controls outside the listbox (#7096)

**Decision:** port

## Problem
`ComboboxContent` carried `role="listbox"`, so the search input and any
`content-top`/`content-bottom` controls were children of the listbox
(axe `aria-required-children`, which the a11y test had disabled).

## Change
- **src/runtime/components/SelectMenu.vue**
  - `useId()`-based `triggerId` (falls back when no `id`) and `listboxId`.
  - Trigger gets `:id="triggerId"` and `aria-controls="listboxId"`.
  - `ComboboxContent` strips `role`, `aria-orientation`, `aria-multiselectable`.
  - `ComboboxInput` gets `aria-controls="listboxId"`.
  - The viewport `div` becomes the `role="listbox"` (id, `aria-labelledby`
    trigger, `aria-multiselectable`, `aria-orientation="vertical"`), replacing
    `role="presentation"`.
- **test/components/SelectMenu.spec.ts** — axe runs with no disabled rules;
  added the "listbox semantics" `it.each` (searchInput × multiple).

## Adaptations
- Fork imports differ (no `onScopeDispose`), `B24Input` with fork props, and
  the fork's multi-line `ComboboxInput`/viewport markup; the same attributes are
  applied there.
- Snapshots need regeneration (54 SelectMenu render snapshots change: trigger
  id / aria-controls, listbox role moves to viewport) — not hand-edited.
