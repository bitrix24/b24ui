## Upstream change

31 files, all under `docs/`. Two kinds of change:

1. **Contributor rotation.** `PlaygroundContributors.vue` reorders and partly
   replaces the ten usernames it renders (`noook`, `danielroe` and `smarroufin`
   out; `hywax`, `maximepvrt` and `malik-jouda` in), and the same swap is made by
   hand across the avatar, command-palette, select-menu, input-menu, dropdown-menu,
   editor and page-list examples plus four `.md` files. The `.md` edits also move
   `avatar.loading: lazy` relative to `suffix` in the YAML — reordering, no
   behaviour.
2. **Playground tile styling**, e.g. `PlaygroundInviteTeam.vue` gains
   `color="neutral"`, `variant="subtle"`, `:content="{ position: 'item-aligned' }"`
   and a `px-4 py-3` padding change.

## Why it does not apply

**Twelve of the 31 files do not exist here.** The whole `docs/app/components/playground/`
directory is absent — `PlaygroundAnnouncement`, `Calendar`, `CommandPalette`,
`Contributors`, `Dashboard`, `Empty`, `InviteTeam`, `Notifications`, `Prompt`,
`ReportBug`, `TransferFunds` — along with `theme-studio/view/ThemeStudioViewEditor.vue`.
That is the entire second kind of change, and the source of the contributor list
itself. The directory is not merely missing these files: it does not exist.

**The other 19 exist but carry different content.** Every avatar in this fork's
docs is an organisation account or a local asset:

plus local files such as `/b24ui/avatar/employee.png` and

**Not one of the usernames is present.** Extracting every GitHub handle the commit

## Note

This is a *content* no-op, not a structural one: the 19 files that exist are real
counterparts of upstream's, and a future commit touching their markup will port
normally. Only the avatar identities diverge, and they diverge by an earlier
deliberate decision — this fork does not advertise nuxt/ui's contributors.
