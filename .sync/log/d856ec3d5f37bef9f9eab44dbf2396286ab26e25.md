# Port: feat(InputNumber): add `startingValue` and `allowInvalid` props (#7146)

**Upstream:** `d856ec3d5f37bef9f9eab44dbf2396286ab26e25` (nuxt/ui)
**Decision:** port

## Upstream change
reka-ui 2.11.0's `NumberFieldRoot` has two new props, and upstream lets them through:
- `startingValue`: the value the first increment or decrement sets on an empty field.
  It is clamped to `min` / `max` but not snapped to `step`, and defaults to `min`, or
  `0` when `min` is not set.
- `allowInvalid`: a typed value is kept even when it is out of range or off the `step`
  grid. Stepping still clamps and snaps. The field does not flag the value as invalid.

`InputNumber.vue` adds both to the `Pick<NumberFieldRootProps, …>` and to the
`reactivePick` that feeds `rootProps`. `input-number.md` gets two sections with Soon
badges, after "Step". `InputNumber.spec.ts` gets three cases, none of them snapshots.

## b24ui port
