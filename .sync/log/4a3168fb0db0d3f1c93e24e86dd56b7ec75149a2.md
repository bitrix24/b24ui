## Upstream change

One 34-line entry in `docs/content/templates.yml` plus two screenshots, adding
`nuxt-ui-templates/calendar` to the templates page: an Apple-Calendar-inspired
Nuxt template with day/week/month views, drag and drop and optimistic updates.

Every URL in the entry points at upstream infrastructure —
`github.com/nuxt-ui-templates/calendar`, `calendar-template.nuxt.dev`,
`ui.nuxt.com/assets/…`.

## Why it does not apply

`docs/content/templates.yml` exists here and has the same shape, but it lists
this fork's own templates — `Dashboard` and `Starter`, each in a `nuxt` and a
`vue` variant, all pointing at `github.com/bitrix24/templates-*` and
`bitrix24.github.io`. There is no b24ui calendar template, and nothing anywhere
in this repository references one.

Porting the entry would put a card on our templates page linking to a
repository this project does not own, with a preview image we do not host.

The two screenshots (`calendar-dark.png`, `calendar-light.png`, ~600 KB
together) are upstream's renders of upstream's template; there is nothing to
mirror them from.

If b24ui ever ships a calendar template, this entry is a reasonable shape to
copy — but that is authoring a template, not porting a commit.
