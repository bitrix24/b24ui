# No-op: fix(Marquee): use logical properties and drop broken vertical RTL animation

**Upstream:** `9b08a84f1d00493681cdae4f883d3a241b70a761` (nuxt/ui, #6833)
**Decision:** no-op — this fork has no Marquee

## Upstream change

Two fixes to `Marquee`, both RTL:

- the horizontal overlay used physical `left-0`/`right-0` and fixed gradient
  directions, so under RTL the fade sat on the wrong edge and pointed the wrong
  way — now `start-0`/`end-0` with `rtl:` gradient counterparts;
- the vertical orientation had an `rtl:` animation pointing at a
  `marquee-vertical-rtl` keyframe whose `to` step read
  `calc(-100% * var(--gap))` — a multiplication where the `from` step has an
  addition, so it was never the mirror of anything. The keyframe is deleted and
  the `rtl:` animation with it: vertical scrolling has no reading direction to
  mirror.

## Why it does not apply here

`Marquee` was never ported into this fork. Confirmed on all four surfaces the
commit touches:

- `src/runtime/components/Marquee.vue` — absent;
- `src/theme/marquee.ts` — absent;
- `src/runtime/keyframes.css` — no `marquee`, `marquee-vertical` or
  `marquee-vertical-rtl` keyframes at all (this file diverged from upstream when
  `735b264` was ported);
- `docs/app/pages/index.vue` and `PageLogos` — neither uses it.

The only two mentions of the word in the tree are deliberate exclusions:
`src/runtime/types/theme.ts:104` and `playgrounds/nuxt/app/composables/useNavigation.ts:63`
both carry it commented out.

Nothing to port and nothing to record beyond this entry — but the RTL reasoning
is worth keeping, since a future Marquee port should start from the fixed
version rather than replay the defect.
