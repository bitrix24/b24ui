## The closest call, and why it is still n/a

`navigation.json.get.ts` adds `'to', 'target'` to the navigation fields, and our
copy **does** carry upstream's pre-image line. The hunk applies cleanly.

It is still n/a, because it is the *consumer* half of a schema addition made in
the same commit. Upstream's `docs` collection schema gains:

```ts
// External navigation entries (e.g. the Figma page): the sidebar link
// points at `to` instead of the page's own path.
to: z.string().optional(),
target: z.string().optional(),
```

Verified against the pre-image rather than inferred: those lines are `+` in this
commit's diff, and the surrounding hunk is the `docs` collection.

This fork's `docs` schema has no page-level `to`/`target`, and no external-link
doc pages — our `figma` collection is commented out in `content.config.ts`. So
requesting the fields would ask the navigation query for something no page can
set: dead config that reads like a feature.

**A near-miss worth recording.** `content.config.ts` *does* define `to` and
`target` at lines 20–21 and 32–33, which makes the change look applicable on a
grep. They belong to the nested `Button` and `PageFeature` schemas, not to the
page frontmatter — and the `to:`/`target:` pairs found in `docs/content/docs/`
are inside `::card-group` blocks in page bodies, not frontmatter either. Reading
the collection schema rather than trusting the grep is what separated the two.
